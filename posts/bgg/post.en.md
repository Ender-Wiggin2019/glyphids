# A one-hour solo puzzle game inspired by SETI

**Spoiler warning:** this game draws on an alien species from *SETI: Space Agencies*. If you want to discover the expansion's aliens for yourself, please be cautious about playing.

I made **Glypyids**, a short solo browser puzzle. Study strange pictures, annotate symbols, and piece together a reply to someone beyond Earth.

SETI fans may recognize a few familiar details. You don't need to have played it to enjoy the encounter.

About **one hour**, in **English or Chinese**. I'd love to hear how your first contact goes.

[Play Glypyids](https://glyphids.ender-wiggin.com/)

An independent fan game, not an official CGE release.
